The Genesis

Born in the crucible of adversity

Caleb Jakes isn’t just a speaker; he is a testament to the undeniable power of surrender. Having navigated profound personal trials, Caleb discovered that true strength isn't found in avoiding pain, but in allowing it to forge purpose.

With over 6 years of experience commanding stages across the nation, he blends the raw authenticity of his journey with strategic, actionable wisdom.
