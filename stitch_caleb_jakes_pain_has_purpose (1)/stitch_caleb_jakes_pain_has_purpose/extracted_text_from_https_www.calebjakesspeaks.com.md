## Certified Motivational Speaker for Schools, Faith, and Conferences

Caleb Jakes is an IAPO-certified motivational speaker and RTF deliverance minister based in Rochester, NY, with six years of professional speaking experience and the founder of Joyionaire™ Enterprises, established in 2023. He speaks internationally to high school and college audiences, male empowerment and resilience conferences, faith-based gatherings, and organizations pursuing transformation through personal development and growth mindset principles. Drawing from real-life stories, biblical insight, and practical strategies for personal growth, Caleb delivers messages that move people from where they are to where they are called to be. His dual certifications position him uniquely to serve both secular institutional environments and faith-based communities with equal credibility and depth. School administrators, conference organizers, church officials, and speaker bureau representatives consistently find in Caleb a voice that is warm, credible, and results-driven.
